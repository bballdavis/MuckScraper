RSS feeds

General ingestion runs every fetch. Right and left enrichment feeds backfill bias-skewed stories. Everything in an Op Ed feed goes to the Op/Ed section, never the news. A URL can sit in more than one bucket. A fallback feed (e.g. a Google News site: search) is fetched instead whenever the feed answers with an error.

Scan order

The order each run fetches the feeds in every bucket, and the order they're listed below. With most reliable first, the better-sourced feeds are in even when a run is cut short. A feed's grade comes from its label or the outlet its articles come in under; a new feed is ungraded until its first fetch.
